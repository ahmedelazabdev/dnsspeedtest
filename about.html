About Our Benchmarking Architecture

Empowering internet users worldwide with transparent, client-side network diagnostics and unbiased DNS telemetry.

Our Core Mission

DNS Speed Test was created to address a critical blind spot in personal network performance: default ISP resolver degradation. The majority of internet subscribers remain connected to recursive resolvers provided by their local Internet Service Providers. These servers are routinely saturated, geographically suboptimal, vulnerable to DNS hijacking, and frequently used to compile commercial browsing profiles.

We believe everyday users deserve access to the same high-precision network diagnostic tools used by network administrators, directly inside their browsers without installing software.

Scientific Client-Side Methodology

Unlike synthetic benchmarking utilities that execute tests from distant cloud datacenters (like AWS, Azure, or GCP), our tool executes 100% inside your local web browser:

  • Local Peering Accuracy: Probes originate directly from your physical connection through your local ISP, ensuring that routing hops and geographic latency are genuinely measured.
  • RFC 8484 Binary Wireformat: We construct standard binary DNS query packets using the ?dns= wireformat standard, ensuring native compatibility across all global public resolvers.
  • TCP/TLS Socket Priming: We perform an initial warmup probe prior to measurement to establish TLS negotiation, eliminating cold-start bias from final latency metrics.
  • Anti-Cache Randomization: Every query dispatches an un-cached challenge to bypass intermediate proxies and force real-world authoritative transit.
  • Comprehensive Latency Metrics: We present Min, Median (P50), Average, and Max values to expose resolver jitter and network variance.

Independent & Non-Commercial Neutrality

We maintain total technical and financial independence. We do not accept sponsored rankings, affiliate payouts, or preferential indexing from any DNS provider. Rankings are generated mathematically based purely on Round-Trip Time (RTT) in milliseconds.
