About Our Benchmarking Architecture

Empowering internet users worldwide with transparent, client-side network diagnostics.

Our Core Mission

DNS Speed Test was founded on a simple principle: internet users deserve total transparency into their network routing performance. The overwhelming majority of internet consumers remain locked into the default recursive resolvers operated by their local Internet Service Providers (ISPs). These ISP servers are routinely under-provisioned, vulnerable to DNS cache poisoning attacks, and frequently used to collect and monetize browsing records.

Scientific Client-Side Methodology

Unlike server-side synthetic testing tools that measure resolver latency from static cloud datacenters (such as AWS us-east-1), our benchmarking tool executes 100% inside your local web browser:

  • Local Peering Accuracy: Probes originate directly from your physical device through your local ISP, reflecting authentic transit and peering hops.
  • TCP/TLS Warm-Up Protocol: We execute an un-timed pre-flight probe to establish TLS handshakes and socket reuse, eliminating cold-start bias from final latency calculations.
  • Anti-Cache Randomization: Every query dispatches an un-cached random challenge subdomain (e.g., bench-x8a92.cloudflare.com) to force authentic round-trip resolution.
  • Jitter & Statistical Stability: We measure consecutive probe delta variations, highlighting servers prone to latency spikes.

Independent & Unbiased Testing

We do not accept financial compensation, paid placements, or sponsorship from any DNS provider. All benchmark results are ranked strictly on measured Round-Trip Times (RTT) in milliseconds.
