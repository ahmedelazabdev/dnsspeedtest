About DNS Speed Test

An open, neutral network diagnostic utility helping everyday users discover the fastest DNS resolvers for their location.

Our Mission

The DNS Speed Test initiative was launched to empower internet users worldwide to make data-driven decisions about their network configuration. Most internet subscribers default to the DNS resolvers provided by their local ISPs, which are frequently slow, congested, and vulnerable to outages and aggressive advertising injection.

Benchmarking Methodology

Unlike synthetic server-side tests that measure latency from remote datacenters, our benchmark executes directly inside your web browser:

  • Real Client-Side Measurements: Probes are sent directly from your unique local ISP connection and geographical location.
  • DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) API Probes: We leverage official DoH endpoints provided by Cloudflare, Google, Quad9, AdGuard, and Alibaba.
  • Anti-Caching Randomization: Every probe request utilizes a unique, randomized sub-domain challenge to bypass local browser caches and measure genuine network transit time.

Technical Independence

We maintain complete neutrality. We are not owned by, affiliated with, or funded by any DNS service provider. Rankings are generated purely on measured mathematical latency (Round-Trip Time) in milliseconds.
