Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

We believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Here is our commitment to zero logging.

1. Zero Server-Side Logging & Client Isolation

DNS Speed Test executes exclusively inside your local web browser sandbox. We do not maintain server-side infrastructure to store, inspect, log, or track your IP address, geographical coordinates, benchmark telemetry, or DNS resolution results.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Cookies (Google AdSense)

To provide free access to this tool, we may display non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdSense:

  • Google and its advertising partners use cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visit history across the web.
  • You can opt out of personalized interest-based advertising at any time through Google Ads Settings or through the Network Advertising Initiative.

3. Third-Party DoH Endpoints

When initiating a speed benchmark, your web browser transmits HTTPS requests directly to public DoH APIs operated by independent entities (such as Cloudflare, Google, Quad9, and AdGuard). Each provider handles inbound lookup requests in accordance with their respective public privacy disclosures.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users possess the right to data sovereignty. Because this application processes zero personally identifiable information (PII) on our servers, no user data is collected, sold, or retained.
