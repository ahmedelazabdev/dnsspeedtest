Privacy Policy & Terms

Your privacy is paramount. Discover how we protect your network data and maintain complete anonymity.

1. Client-Side Execution & Zero Logging

DNS Speed Test operates as a strictly client-side application running inside your web browser. We do not record, transmit, store, or profile your personal IP address, browsing activity, or benchmark results on any centralized server. Probe queries are sent directly from your device to the respective public DNS-over-HTTPS providers.

2. Cookies & Advertising (Google AdSense)

We do not deploy invasive tracking cookies. To maintain this service as a free, publicly accessible utility, we may partner with compliant advertising networks such as Google AdSense:

  • Google utilizes cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve relevant advertisements based on user visits to this and other websites.
  • You can opt out of personalized interest-based advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Third-Party DNS Endpoints

When you initiate a benchmark, your browser sends standardized DNS resolution requests to public DoH endpoints operated by third parties (such as Cloudflare, Google, and Quad9). Each of these external providers maintains its own independent privacy and logging policy governing inbound requests.

4. Compliance (GDPR & CCPA)

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), our architecture is designed with privacy-by-default. Because we collect zero personally identifiable information (PII), your online identity remains fully protected.
