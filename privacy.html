Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

Your privacy is an absolute priority. Learn how our architecture ensures zero data storage and complete anonymity.

1. Zero Server-Side Logging & Client Isolation

DNS Speed Test operates strictly within your local web browser environment. We do not host intermediate servers to intercept, inspect, record, log, or track your IP address, network queries, or benchmark results. All network requests are dispatched directly from your browser sandbox to public DNS-over-HTTPS endpoints.

2. Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense)

To maintain this tool as a free public utility, we may display compliant advertisements provided by advertising partners such as Google AdSense:

  • Google and third-party vendors use cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to deliver contextual and interest-based advertisements based on visits across the web.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by configuring your preferences at Google Ads Settings.

3. Third-Party DNS Resolvers

When you execute a speed benchmark, your browser sends standardized DNS resolution queries directly to public DoH APIs managed by independent organizations (such as Cloudflare, Google, Quad9, AdGuard, and Cisco OpenDNS). Each provider processes incoming requests in accordance with their respective privacy charters and retention policies.

4. Global Privacy Compliance (GDPR & CCPA)

In strict adherence to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), our software operates under a Privacy-by-Design standard. Because we collect zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII), your browsing profile and geographic identity remain entirely anonymous.
